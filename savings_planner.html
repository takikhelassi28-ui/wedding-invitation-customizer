Impulse Shield

The 30-Day Cool Down Rule

When you feel the urge to buy something non-essential, you MUST write it down here first. Wait exactly 30 days. If you still want the item and can afford it, you may purchase it. Often, the urge disappears, and you can redirect the cost of the item straight into your savings jars!

Daily Habiteer

Micro-Savings & HP Quest

Daily HP Tracker

A gamified calendar designed to anchor savings habits. Every day, you start with 5 Health Points (hearts). Every time you spend money on a non-essential impulse item, cross off one heart. Fill in the No-Spend checkbox if you spend nothing beyond your fixed needs, and note down your daily micro-savings ($1, $5, or $10 saved from choosing not to buy coffee/snacks).
